# Landing device

Three independent, visual puzzles open the two route hatches together. The solution is visible on each panel, and there is no prescribed order.

- Central axes: match right, left, down. Each click rotates only its own axis.
- Left circuit panel: rotate all three breaks into horizontal connections.
- Right alignment panel: place the sliders at the marked 30 and 70 positions, with a tolerance of five.

Changing one panel does not reset the others. Three indicator lights show each panel's status. Keyboard buttons and native sliders correspond to the drawn controls.

Landing audio is fixed to `threshold` (TRACK 09). The window exposes playback and volume only. The audio engine rejects other track selections when configured with `fixedTrack`, including a previously saved game soundtrack preference.
